## Structure

**Org-wide (T1) — flat files at repo root:**
- `intent.md` — org mission and goals
- `constraints.md` — org-wide rules, compliance, hard limits
- `decisions.md` — cross-team decisions
- `agents.md` — AI navigation

T1 content always goes in one of these four files. There are no T1 subfolders. Do not create `intent/`, `constraints/`, or `decisions/` as directories.

**Discipline folders (T2) — one folder per discipline:**
- `product/` — product strategy, decisions, and context
- `engineering/` — engineering strategy, decisions, and context
- `sales/` — sales strategy, decisions, and context
- `marketing/` — marketing strategy, decisions, and context
- `customer-success/` — customer-success strategy, decisions, and context
- `finance/` — finance strategy, decisions, and context
- `operations/` — operations strategy, decisions, and context
- `people/` — people strategy, decisions, and context
